Who is responsible

Layoutly is made by Alexandru Iordache, an independent developer based in Romania, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: contact.iordache@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Layoutly runs on your computer. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • The desktop app sends data in one case only: official builds check your license key with Lemon Squeezy.
  • This website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and serves its own fonts.
  • We never sell or rent personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

What the app keeps on your computer

The app keeps one settings file in its data folder on your computer (for example ~/Library/Application Support/Layoutly on macOS). It holds:

  • your inspect shortcut, your stack and your AI assistant, and whether you finished onboarding;
  • the last website you opened;
  • today's free usage: the date, and which components and sites you used, so repeating one doesn't count twice;
  • if you bought a license: the key, its activation ID, and when it was activated and last checked.

Websites you open in the built-in browser keep their own cookies, cache and storage in that same folder, as in any browser. Components, screenshots and styles you extract stay in the app's memory and go to your clipboard when you copy them. Nothing of this leaves your computer unless you paste it somewhere. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes all of it.

What the app sends, and when

  • License checks (official builds only). When you activate a key, the app sends the key and a device name made of your computer's name and operating system (for example “Alex's MacBook (darwin)”) to the Lemon Squeezy license API, so the activation can be counted and managed. Each time the app starts, it sends the key and the activation ID to confirm the license is still valid. Deactivating in Settings sends them once more. Lemon Squeezy also sees your IP address, as with any internet request.
  • The websites you open. The built-in browser loads them directly. Those sites receive your request, IP address and cookies exactly as they would from any browser, under their own privacy policies.
  • Links you click. “Copy & open” opens your AI assistant, and the Contact buttons open your email app or X, in your own apps. Layoutly sends nothing to them; you decide what to paste or write.

The open-source build makes no license checks and sends nothing at all.

Buying a license

Licenses are sold by Lemon Squeezy (Lemon Squeezy, LLC, a Stripe company), which acts as the merchant of record: it takes the payment, issues the invoice, handles sales tax and VAT, and emails you the license key. Lemon Squeezy handles your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card.

From each order we receive the details Lemon Squeezy shares with sellers: your name, email address, country and the order itself. We use them only to deliver your license, support you, process refunds and keep the records the law requires.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail). We use your message only to answer it.

This website

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service running and secure, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • To show the project's star count, your browser asks the GitHub API for it, so GitHub receives that request.
  • Download buttons lead to GitHub Releases, which serves the installers.
  • Fonts, images and scripts are served from this site. The live demo runs entirely in your browser.
  • No cookies, no local storage, no analytics, no advertising or social trackers.

Why we may use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide what you asked for (the license, its activation and checks, support for customers): performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b).
  • To keep accounting and tax records of orders: legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c).
  • To answer other messages and keep the website secure: legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f).

How long we keep it

  • Order details: as long as your license exists, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Activation records at Lemon Squeezy: until you deactivate the device or the license ends.
  • Support emails: until the conversation is over and no longer needed, or sooner if you ask.
  • Data on your computer: until you delete it.

Who else receives it

Only the service providers named above, each for its own part: Lemon Squeezy (sales and licenses), GitHub (website and downloads) and Google (email). Some of them process data in the United States; where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to contact.iordache@gmail.com; we answer within one month. We may need to confirm it's you, for example by replying from the email used for the order.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: the one where you live or work, or Romania's ANSPDCP.

Children, security and changes

Layoutly is not directed at children under 16. We keep what we hold to the minimum described here and protect access to it. We make no decisions about you by automated means.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a change affects how we use data we already hold, we'll tell customers by email before it applies.
