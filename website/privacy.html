Privacy

Layoutly runs on your computer. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. The only thing the app sends anywhere is your license key, to check it.

Last updated September 28, 2026

What stays on your computer

Everything you do in Layoutly happens locally. The app keeps a small settings file in its own data folder on your computer, with:

  • your inspect key, your stack and your AI assistant;
  • the last website you opened;
  • how many free components and styles you used today, so the daily allowance can reset at midnight;
  • your license key and its activation ID, if you bought one.

Websites you open in the built-in browser keep their cookies and cache in that same folder, like in any browser. Components, screenshots and styles you copy go to your clipboard and nowhere else.

What leaves your computer

  • License checks (official builds only). When you activate a key, Layoutly sends it to the Lemon Squeezy license API together with a device name made of your computer's name and operating system, so you can see and manage your activations. When the app starts, it sends the key and the activation ID to confirm the license is still valid. Deactivating from Settings sends them once more.
  • The websites you visit. The built-in browser loads them directly, so those sites receive your request the way they would from any browser.
  • Your AI assistant. “Copy & open” copies the result and opens your AI in your default browser. Layoutly sends nothing to it; you paste.

The open-source build makes no license checks at all.

Buying a license

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record, under its own privacy policy. Layoutly never sees your card details. We receive the order details Lemon Squeezy shares with sellers, such as your email address, and use them only to deliver and support your license.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits as described in GitHub's privacy statement. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and Fontshare and reads the project's star count from the GitHub API. We use no cookies and no analytics.
